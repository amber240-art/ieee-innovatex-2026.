Pioneering Engineering at MITS Gwalior

Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (Estd. 1957) • Joint IEEE IAS & RAS Student Chapter Initiative.

Bridging Theory with Industrial Reality

The IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS) and IEEE Robotics & Automation Society (RAS) Student Branch Chapters at Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS Gwalior) serve as a premier launchpad for engineering students.

The Challenge Pillar

Encouraging students to interrogate difficult research problems in automation, robotic vision, power electronics, and edge computing through IEEE student paper challenges.

The Create Pillar

Moving beyond simulations into tangible cyber-physical systems with microcontrollers, ROS 2 nodes, and industrial protocols in state-of-the-art campus labs.

The Contribute Pillar

Providing leadership roles, chapter officer positions, conference travel grants, and lifelong alumni mentorship across high-tech industries.
